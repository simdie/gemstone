Here you will find a selection of jewellery made by us for our clients, as well as jewellery made by clients using our gemstones. Many thanks to our clients for allowing us to photograph their jewellery.

All our jewellery is made using recycled gold; no freshly-mined metal is used. The use of gold that is already in circulation ensures that neither the mercury nor cyanide that are commonly used in mining are necessary. Thus, neither the health of workers nor the environment are endangered.
